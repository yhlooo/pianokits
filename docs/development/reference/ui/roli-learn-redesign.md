# 参考：ROLI Learn App 2025 视觉改版（ebony & ivory 配色）

- 来源：https://roli.com/blog/the-roli-learn-app-is-getting-a-new-look
- 抓取日期：2026-09-05
- 性质：竞品（钢琴学习应用）官方改版说明，原文直接摘录，未加主观加工。

## 关键摘录（原文）

> Once inside the ROLI Learn app, you'll notice our biggest visual update yet. The
> interface now features a new **ebony and ivory palette** — a nod to the piano keys,
> and a design choice that helps you focus where it matters most.

> This shift from the previous blue ROLI Learn app theme brings:
>
> 1. A calmer, less cluttered feel when browsing lessons
> 2. Clearer contrast between different content types
> 3. Better visibility in lesson stages and learning paths

> Many of you told us that the home screen could feel a little overwhelming at times,
> with so much great content to explore. So we've refined the layout and colors to help
> you navigate more naturally, stay focused on your current lesson, and enjoy a smoother
> overall experience.

## 要点

- 改版方向：从"蓝色主题"转向**乌木（ebony）+ 象牙（ivory）**——直接呼应钢琴黑白键；
- 目标感受：更平静（calmer）、更少杂乱（less cluttered）、对比更清晰；
- 发布方：ROLI Team，2025-10-10。
